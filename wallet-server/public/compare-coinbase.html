Fizzl and Coinbase for agents

Checked 23 October 2026, from Coinbase's own documentation (sources below). Coinbase's products change; their docs have the latest.

On 22 October Coinbase launched a page inviting people to connect ChatGPT, Grok, Claude and other agents to their Coinbase account. Good news for agent payments. Coinbase has two agent products, and the Fizzl Agent Wallet does part of the same job in a different way. Here is how they differ.

Which one fits

Coinbase for Agents, when your agent should trade on your Coinbase account, and Coinbase offers it in your country.
Coinbase Wallet MCP, when you want to approve every single payment yourself, on Base.
Fizzl Agent Wallet, when your agent should pay for APIs on its own, within a daily budget, with a check before every signature, on more than one network, wherever you live.

They also work together: Coinbase for trading, the Fizzl Agent Wallet for paying APIs.
