Paste this into Claude, Cursor or any agent. It reads the steps and walks you through them:

Connect to wallet.fizzl.eu/skill.md

You stay in charge: it never asks you to paste a private key into the chat. Read what your agent will read

An agent is your own AI assistant or bot, for example Claude Desktop, Cursor or a script of yours. Add it here and it gets a key: with that key it pays through this wallet, within your rules.

One agent can use every service, including all four Fizzl services below. You don't add services as agents. More agents only help to keep assistants apart, so you can pause or remove one on its own.

FIZZL SERVICES YOUR AGENTS CAN USE

Paid services, not agents to add. Your agent calls them and pays per call in USDC through this wallet, so your price rule and daily budget apply, and every purchase shows up as a receipt.

Your agent can also find services itself: ask it to "find a paid API for a bitcoin trend signal under $0.10 and buy one". The wallet MCP (version 0.4 or newer) searches the public x402 catalog, and your rules still apply.
