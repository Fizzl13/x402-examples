2. What the service is, and isn't

The service lets you set rules for what your AI agents may spend, asks you to approve anything above those rules (on the dashboard or on Telegram), and keeps receipts and an activity log. Agents connect with their own key and ask us before they sign. They then sign and pay themselves.

  • Not a wallet, bank or custodian. We never ask for, see or store your private keys or recovery phrase, and we never hold or move your funds, except the Pro payments you send us yourself.
  • Not financial advice. Nothing in the service tells you what to buy or sell.
  • Safety checks are a help, not a guarantee. Requests are checked by presign-guard, which flags known risks such as drainers, sanctioned addresses and unlimited approvals. It can miss new threats and it can be wrong. A "green" verdict doesn't mean a payment is safe or worth it.
  • When in doubt, it stops. If our server can't be reached, agents that use it stop signing. That is by design, so your agents may be unable to pay while the service is down.

5. Paying

  • Payments are blockchain transactions. They can't be reversed by us or anyone else. Pay from the wallet you signed in with, in USDC, on a network the dashboard offers for that wallet (Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon or Ethereum for an Ethereum wallet; Solana for a Solana wallet), to the address the dashboard shows. Choose the network you pay on before you claim a payment. Network fees (gas) are yours.
  • A payment counts once it is confirmed on-chain and our server has checked it. If a payment doesn't show up, use "Paid, but it didn't show up?" on the dashboard or contact us with the transaction hash. Payments sent to the wrong address, on the wrong network or in the wrong token can't be recovered by us.

6. Automatic payment

  • Automatic payment is available for Ethereum wallets, on Base. If you turn it on, you approve our subscription contract on Base for at most 12 months of Pro ($60). From then on, $5 is taken from your wallet every 30 days, until you turn it off or the approval is used up. The contract can only send $5 per 30 days, only to our address. Its code is public.
  • We remind you on Telegram (if connected) 3 days before each payment.
  • If a payment can't go through, for example because your approval is used up or you don't have enough USDC on Base, Pro keeps working for 3 days. After that you're on Free. Nothing is deleted, and extra agents are paused.
  • You can turn automatic payment off at any time on the dashboard, or by setting your approval to 0 in your wallet. Time you already paid for stays.
