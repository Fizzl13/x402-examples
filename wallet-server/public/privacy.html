Privacy statement

Fizzl Agent Wallet (wallet.fizzl.eu) · last updated 3 October 2026 (website counter added)

3. Cookies and local storage

  • aw_session: a signed cookie that keeps you signed in, for 7 days. It is strictly necessary, so no consent banner is needed.
  • aw_wallet (local storage, only in your browser): which wallet you signed in with, so the right one opens when you pay.

No third-party analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, and no cookies for statistics: the anonymous counts above are made on our own server. Even the fonts are served from this server.

3a. The Fizzl websites (fizzl.eu and its subdomains)

fizzl.eu, ai.fizzl.eu, projects.fizzl.eu, cv.fizzl.eu and lab.fizzl.eu count visits with a small script from this server: which page was opened, and clicks from those pages to this wallet or a Fizzl service, with the time and the kind of browser (e.g. "browser" or a bot's name). No cookies, no local storage, no IP address, nothing that identifies you, and nothing at all when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. A link to this wallet carries the name of the site you came from (?ref=fizzl.eu), so we can see whether the website leads to sign-ups. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)); kept at most 2 years, like the other anonymous statistics.

4. What we never do

  • We never ask for, see or store your private key or recovery phrase, and we never hold your funds. Your agents sign and pay themselves.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, don't use it for advertising or profiling, and don't use it to train AI models.

5. Who helps us (processors)

  • Render (hosting, EU region Frankfurt). Render may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for a limited time.
  • Upstash (the database that stores the data above).
  • GitHub (a private repository that holds the anonymous usage statistics).
  • Telegram, only if you connect it. Messages go through Telegram, under Telegram's privacy policy.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla, only if you turn on notifications on a device: they deliver the notification to that device. The message is encrypted end to end; they can't read it.
  • RPC providers for Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Ethereum and Solana, to read public blockchain data about payments.

Render and Upstash are companies based in the United States. Where data is processed outside the European Economic Area, this happens under the safeguards these providers offer, such as the EU standard contractual clauses.

6. The blockchain is public

Pro payments happen on a public blockchain (Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Ethereum or Solana), automatic payment on Base. Your address, the amounts and the times are public there and permanent. That is how blockchains work, and nobody, including us, can delete them.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to get it in a portable format, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to ask us to restrict processing.

  • Delete: on the dashboard, under "Your account", use Delete my account. Everything above goes, except the payment records we must keep. Turn off automatic payment first: it lives on the blockchain, so only your wallet can stop it.
  • Anything else, such as a copy of your data: write to {{CONTACT}}. We answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. We'd appreciate hearing from you first.

9. Security

  • Agent keys are stored only as hashes.
  • Sessions are signed and HTTP-only, and everything runs over HTTPS.
  • Every account can see and change only its own data.
  • Approvals on Telegram are accepted only from the Telegram user linked to that account.

10. Age

The service is not meant for anyone under 16.

11. Changes

If we change this statement in a way that matters to you, we'll say so on the dashboard (and on Telegram, if you connected it) before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
