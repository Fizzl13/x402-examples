Your wallet in ChatGPT, Claude and Grok

Add one URL to the AI app you use, and it can find paid APIs for you and ask you to pay for them. You sign every payment yourself, in your own wallet. Fizzl never holds a key.

https://wallet.fizzl.eu/mcp

Add it to your app

Claude (claude.ai, desktop, iPhone and Android)
  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it "Fizzl Wallet", paste the URL above, add it, then Connect.
  3. Sign in to your Fizzl wallet and tap Allow.
  4. In a chat, turn it on under + → Connectors. A connector added on the web also works in the phone app.
ChatGPT
  1. Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings → turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors: Create, paste the URL, sign-in method OAuth.
  3. Sign in to your Fizzl wallet and tap Allow.
  4. In a chat, choose the connector under +.
Grok and other apps

Wherever an app lets you add a remote MCP server with sign-in, use the same URL. The app's name shows on the page where you allow it.

The apps rename their menus now and then; look for "custom connector", "developer mode" or "MCP". Not signed in to Fizzl yet? The sign-in page lets you start free.

What the app can do

  • See your wallet's status and the limits you set.
  • Search the x402 catalog for paid APIs (weather, prices, data, safety checks and more), with each seller's payout record from x402 Doctor. Sellers that take USDC on Base or Solana are listed.
  • Check a text it received for prompt-injection traps, for free.
  • Ask you for a payment, which you sign. The answer then goes to the app.

How a payment works

  1. The app finds an API and asks to pay it. Before you're asked, the seller's payout address is checked against the OFAC sanctions list and looked up in x402 Doctor, and your balance is checked.
  2. You get a link: in the chat, on Telegram if you linked it, and as a notification in the installed app.
  3. You see the price, the seller and the checks, and sign in your own wallet, or say no.
  4. Fizzl sends the signed payment to the API and gives the answer to the app, checked for hidden instructions. The receipt is on your dashboard.

Disconnect

Each connected app is an agent on your dashboard, named after the app ("ChatGPT connector", "Claude connector"). Remove it there and its access ends at once.

Questions

How do I use the Fizzl Agent Wallet in ChatGPT or Claude?
Add a custom connector with the URL https://wallet.fizzl.eu/mcp, sign in to your Fizzl wallet when the app asks, and allow the app. It can then search paid APIs and ask for payments, which you sign yourself in your own wallet.

Can the app pay without me?
No. Through the connector every payment waits for your signature in your own wallet: USDC on Base (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet or another Ethereum wallet), USDC on Solana (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) or RLUSD on the XRP Ledger (Xaman). Your signature allows exactly that amount to exactly that seller, for a few minutes. Fizzl never holds a key.

What if the API doesn't answer after I sign?
Fizzl sends the request once more with the same signed payment. That payment can settle only once, so you are never charged twice. If the API still gives no answer, Fizzl checks on the chain whether your payment went through: if it did, you get a receipt in Purchases and the request shows as paid without an answer; if not, nothing was paid; if the chain can't tell, it shows as maybe paid. Asking for the same request again within the hour then comes with a warning, so you never sign twice without knowing.

Do I need SOL or ETH for fees?
No ETH on Base and no SOL on Solana: in x402 the seller's facilitator pays the network fee. On the XRP Ledger your Xaman account pays the usual tiny XRP fee.

How do I disconnect an app?
On the dashboard, remove the agent named after the app (for example "ChatGPT connector"). Its access ends at once.

Want an agent that pays on its own within a daily budget instead? Install the MCP server in Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor: see skill.md.
