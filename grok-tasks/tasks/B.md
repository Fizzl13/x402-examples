Daily scam watch on X. Report to me in Dutch, short; post drafts in English.

1. SEARCH
Search X for posts from the last 24 hours that push a token or a claim with a real risk:
- tokens presented as the official token of a known project, company or person (often in replies under big accounts),
- "airdrop", "claim now", "connect wallet to claim" links, especially from new accounts or in replies,
- posts warning about a drainer, rug or fake contract, with the address.
Skip ordinary new meme tokens (Solana addresses ending in "pump") unless the post claims they are official for a known name.
Do at least 4 different X searches (at least one for each kind above) before you conclude there is nothing.

- Token with a contract address: token_quick_verdict (presign-guard) with the right chain, for every such token you found, up to 5.

Report "domein klopt" or "domein wijkt af (officieel: …)". injection_quick_check only checks whether text tries to steer an AI agent; a green there does not mean the site is safe, so never call a claim site safe.
Never open a claim link in a wallet, never connect, sign or pay.
